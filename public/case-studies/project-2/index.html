01

Problem

Important emails were getting buried, repetitive tasks were being processed manually, and admin work was taking valuable time every day. The team needed a reliable way to turn incoming emails into actionable tasks without constantly checking and organizing everything manually.

02

Solution

Built an AI Admin Assistant in n8n that monitors Gmail, extracts tasks with OpenAI, removes sensitive data, detects duplicates, sends urgent alerts, and logs everything automatically.

03

Result

Instead of starting the day by digging through an inbox, the team gets a structured and prioritized task list automatically. Less manual processing. Fewer duplicate tasks. Better data protection. Faster response to urgent work. The biggest improvement wasn’t just AI extracting information. It was building safeguards around the automation so only relevant, valid tasks reached the final system.
